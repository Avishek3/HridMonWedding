Our Story

It started in a quiet year, when the world stood still,
Two souls met online, as though by fate’s will.
Miles apart, yet hearts so near,
Sharing songs, dreams, and a wish to be here.

Pink Floyd played, Wish You Were Here,
And every note pulled you closer, my dear.
Screens lit up with laughter and late-night calls,
We built a world with no borders, no walls.

A year went by, yet we had not met,
But love had bloomed — how could we forget?
When we finally saw each other face to face,
It felt like home, that warm embrace.

Through flights and time zones, through joy and tears,
We held on tight, silencing fears.
From strangers to lovers, to partners for life,
From “Will you be mine?” to “Will you be my wife?”
Now we stand together, heart in heart,
Two souls that destiny refused to keep apart.
Our story is simple, but ours to keep,
A promise to cherish, through valleys deep.

And when the world asks, “Was it love from the start?”
We’ll smile and say, it was written in our heart.

Events

4th December

Aiburobhaat & Mehendi 10AM onwards

Aiburobhaat is the traditional “last meal” before the bride ties the knot — and trust us, this isn’t just any meal! Expect an extravagant Bengali spread with ilish maachh, chingri, kosha mangsho, bhaat, dal, and mishti galore. It’s our way of saying goodbye to bachelor life with a full tummy and a happy heart! It is followed by Mehendi, where the bride’s hands get adorned with beautiful henna designs — a symbol of love and happiness. Add to that a full-on Sangeet night where we sing, dance, and celebrate the couple in true Bollywood-meets-Bengali style. Come prepared to dance till you drop!

5th December

Gaaye Holud 10AM onwards

This ceremony is usually held separately for the bride and the groom. This one is for the bride. It is a low-key event that involves the application of turmeric paste on the hands, legs and face of the bride. The groom is not allowed to see the bride after this ritual until the final wedding ceremony starts.

Biye 6PM - 12AM

This is the main event in the entire Season 2 — our Biye! Watch the groom arrive with a borjatri (wedding procession), the bride’s grand entry with shubho drishti (the first look), the sacred saat paak (circling the groom), and sindoor daan (vermillion ceremony). It’s a magical blend of love, laughter, conch shells, and ululations.

8th December

Reception 6PM-12AM

The bride is officially welcomed into her new home with bhaat-kapor and rituals — followed by the grand reception where we all eat, mingle, and celebrate together. Dress your best, bring your appetite, and let’s end the wedding festivities on a delicious note!
